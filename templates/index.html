      How Resume Scorer works

      1. Upload your resume. Drag a PDF into the upload area, or click it to browse your files.
      2. Paste the job description. Include the responsibilities and required skills so the analysis has something to compare against.
      3. Click Analyze resume. You'll get an overall score plus a breakdown across skills, experience, education, and job compliance.
      4. Review your strengths and gaps. Use the suggested improvements to tailor your resume before you apply.
      5. Start a new analysis anytime to score another resume or role.
